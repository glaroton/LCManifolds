# LCManifolds

**Manifolds modeled on locally convex spaces, formalized in Lean 4 and Mathlib.**

API documentation (generated by doc-gen4): <https://glaroton.github.io/LCManifolds/docs/>

> **Status: early stage.** At present the library contains manifolds, smooth maps, the tangent
> bundle, tangent maps, and vector fields with the Lie bracket. It is the geometric companion of [LCAnalysis](https://github.com/glaroton/LCAnalysis), which provides the
> calculus.

## Contents

* [`LCManifolds/Basic.lean`](LCManifolds/Basic.lean):
  * `mbGroupoid E`, the structure groupoid of Michal–Bastiani smooth diffeomorphisms between open
    subsets of a locally convex space `E`;
  * `MBManifold`, smooth manifolds modeled on `E` [Nee06, Def. I.3.1; GN26, Ch. 3].
* [`LCManifolds/Product.lean`](LCManifolds/Product.lean): products. `M × N` is a manifold
  modeled on `E × F` (`Prod.mbManifold`); the projections are smooth and pairs of `C^k` maps
  are `C^k`.
* [`LCManifolds/Connection.lean`](LCManifolds/Connection.lean): connections and covariant
  derivatives. A `Connection` is a family of Christoffel symbols `Γᵢ`, one per chart, with the
  change of charts law `Γⱼ(τ x)(dτ a, dτ b) = dτ Γᵢ(x)(a, b) + d²τₓ(a, b)`; conventions as in
  Lang (first argument the direction of motion, Lang's sign for the curvature).
  * `covDeriv`: `∇_X Y = dỸ · X̃ − Γ(X̃, Ỹ)`; `localRep_covDeriv`: it does not depend on the
    chart (the `d²τ` terms cancel);
  * `covDeriv_smulField_left`, `covDeriv_smulField_right`: `∇_{φX} Y = φ ∇_X Y`,
    `∇_X (φY) = X(φ) Y + φ ∇_X Y`;
  * torsion: `∇_X Y − ∇_Y X − [X, Y] = Γ(Y, X) − Γ(X, Y)` pointwise, and
    `∇_X Y − ∇_Y X = [X, Y]` for symmetric connections (sprays);
  * `contMDiffMB_covDeriv`: `∇_X Y` is `C^k` if `X` is `C^k` and `Y` is `C^(k+1)`;
  * `curvature_eq`: **the curvature is tensorial**. `R(X, Y) Z = ∇_X ∇_Y Z − ∇_Y ∇_X Z −
    ∇_[X,Y] Z` (defined through vector fields, so it does not depend on the chart) is, in a chart,
    the trilinear expression `dΓ(v)(u, w) − dΓ(u)(v, w) + Γ(u, Γ(v, w)) − Γ(v, Γ(u, w))` (Lang,
    Prop. IX.1.2); `curvature_swap`, `curvature_bianchi`: skew-symmetry and Bianchi's identity.
* [`LCManifolds/CovariantCurve.lean`](LCManifolds/CovariantCurve.lean): covariant derivatives
  along curves and geodesics.
  * `covDerivAlong`: for a lift `β` of a curve `α`, `D_t β = μ̇ − Γ(α̃)(α̃̇, μ)` in a chart;
    `covDerivAlong_chart`: it does not depend on the chart;
  * `velocity`, `isCkLift_velocity`: the velocity of a `C^(k+1)` curve is a `C^k` lift;
  * `IsGeodesic` (`D_t α' = 0`) and `isGeodesic_iff`: the geodesic equation
    `α̃̈ = Γ(α̃)(α̃̇, α̃̇)` in every chart. Existence of geodesics is not claimed (it needs ODE
    theory, which fails in general locally convex spaces).
* [`LCManifolds/DoubleTangent.lean`](LCManifolds/DoubleTangent.lean): the double tangent bundle
  `TTM = T(TM)`, with charts `((x, v), (u, w))` over charts of `M`.
  * `tangentChart_transition_apply`: the transition maps of `TM` are `(x, v) ↦ (τ x, dτₓ v)`;
  * `ddChart_transition_apply`: those of `TTM` are
    `((x, v), (u, w)) ↦ ((τ x, dτₓ v), (dτₓ u, d²τₓ(u, v) + dτₓ w))`;
  * `flip`, the canonical flip `((x, v), (u, w)) ↦ ((x, u), (v, w))`: `ddChart_flip` shows it
    does not depend on the chart (the transition maps commute with it by Schwarz), and
    `flip_flip` that it is an involution.
* [`LCManifolds/Spray.lean`](LCManifolds/Spray.lean): sprays, by their local parts
  `fᵢ(x, v)` with the change of charts law `fⱼ(τ x, dτ v) = dτ fᵢ(x, v) + d²τₓ(v, v)`.
  * `Connection.toSpray`: a connection gives a quadratic spray, `f(x, v) = Γₓ(v, v)`;
  * `Spray.toConnection`: a quadratic spray gives a symmetric connection, by polarization
    `Γₓ(a, b) = ¼ (f(x, a + b) − f(x, a − b))`, and `toConnection_toSpray` recovers the spray.
    The key facts: a map homogeneous of degree 2 is half its second derivative at `0` on the
    diagonal (`dMB_dMB_zero_of_homogeneous`, no regularity needed), and the second derivative
    of the transition maps polarizes.
* [`LCManifolds/Submanifold.lean`](LCManifolds/Submanifold.lean): embedded submanifolds.
  `N ⊆ M` is embedded with model a closed subspace `S ⊆ E` if every point lies in a slice chart,
  `φ(U ∩ N) = φ(U) ∩ S`; no complement of `S` is required. Then `N` is a manifold modeled on `S`
  (`IsEmbeddedSubmanifold.mbManifold`) and the inclusion is smooth. The key calculus fact is
  `ContDiffMBOn.codRestrict`: a `C^k` map with values in a closed subspace is `C^k` into it.
* [`LCManifolds/SubmanifoldNotions.lean`](LCManifolds/SubmanifoldNotions.lean): weak
  immersions (injective differential), immersions (differential a topological isomorphism onto
  a closed subspace) and split immersions (with a continuous linear left inverse); weakly
  immersed, immersed and initial submanifolds (Glöckner–Neeb, Def. 3.3.22); split submanifolds.
  The inclusion of an embedded submanifold is an immersion and is initial
  (`IsEmbeddedSubmanifold.isInitialSubmanifold_val`, via `ContDiffMBOn.codRestrict`), and a split
  immersion for split submanifolds. The module docstring lists the infinite-dimensional
  subtleties: the cusp (Joris), the figure eight, a topologically embedded weak immersion of `ℓ²`
  that is not initial, unitary orbits, and an initial submanifold that is not an immersion.
* [`LCManifolds/Projector.lean`](LCManifolds/Projector.lean): derive and project (Larotonda,
  *Estructuras*, Teorema *esproyectar*). For an embedded submanifold `X ⊆ F` modeled on `S`, the
  tangent spaces `T_p X ⊆ F` (read in any slice chart), fields of projectors `E : X × F → F`
  onto them (`ProjectorField`), and the connection they define (`ProjectorField.connection`):
  in a slice chart with `ψ = φ⁻¹|_S`, `Γ_y(a, b) = - dφ (E (d²ψ_y(a, b)))`. It is symmetric by
  Schwarz, and the change of charts law follows from a second-order chain rule
  (`dMB_dMB_comp`).
* [`LCManifolds/ContMDiff.lean`](LCManifolds/ContMDiff.lean): `C^k` and smooth maps between
  manifolds (`ContMDiffMBOn`, `SmoothMBMOn`, `SmoothMBM`), defined through the local
  representatives in the preferred charts:
  * `ContMDiffMBOn.contDiffMBOn_chart`: the local representatives in *any* charts of the atlases
    are `C^k`, since transition maps are smooth;
  * `ContMDiffMBOn.comp`: compositions of `C^k` maps are `C^k`;
  * identities and constant maps are smooth, and between the model spaces the notion agrees with
    the Michal–Bastiani one (`contMDiffMBOn_iff_contDiffMBOn`).
* [`LCManifolds/TangentBundle.lean`](LCManifolds/TangentBundle.lean): the tangent bundle.
  * `tangentBundleCore`: a fiber bundle core indexed by the charts of the atlas, whose coordinate
    changes are the derivatives of the transition maps (the cocycle condition is the chain rule);
  * `TangentBundle E M`, a topological fiber bundle with fiber `E`, charted on `E × E`;
  * `TangentBundle.mbManifold`: it is a manifold. Its transition maps are
    `(y, w) ↦ (τ y, dτ(y) w)`, which are smooth because `τ` is.
* [`LCManifolds/TangentMap.lean`](LCManifolds/TangentMap.lean): the tangent map `Tf : TM → TN`.
  * `dMB_chart_comp`, `dMB_comp_chart`: derivatives of local representatives change by the
    derivatives of the transition maps;
  * `tangentMap_chart`: in the charts of the tangent bundles, `Tf` reads `(y, w) ↦ (h y, dh(y) w)`
    for the local representative `h` of `f`;
  * `ContMDiffMB.tangentMap`: if `f` is `C^(k+1)`, then `Tf` is `C^k`;
  * `tangentMap_id`, `tangentMap_comp`: `T id = id` and `T(g ∘ f) = Tg ∘ Tf`.
* [`LCManifolds/VectorField.lean`](LCManifolds/VectorField.lean): vector fields and the Lie
  bracket.
  * `localRep_transition`: local representatives transform by `X̃ⱼ ∘ τ = dτ · X̃ᵢ`;
  * `lieBracket`: defined in the preferred chart at each point by the local formula
    `dỸ · X̃ − dX̃ · Ỹ`;
  * `localRep_lieBracket`: **the bracket does not depend on the chart**. In infinite dimensions
    vector fields need not have flows, so the usual proof through the Lie derivative is not
    available; the proof is direct (`localBracket_transition`): differentiating
    `X̃ⱼ ∘ τ = dτ · X̃ᵢ` produces `d²τ(X̃ᵢ, Ỹᵢ)`, which cancels by the symmetry of second
    derivatives;
  * `lieBracket_swap`: antisymmetry;
  * `contMDiffMB_of_localRep`: a vector field with `C^k` local representatives is `C^k` as a map
    `M → TM`; `contMDiffMB_lieBracket`: if `X, Y` are `C^(k+1)`, then `[X, Y]` is `C^k`;
  * `lieBracket_jacobi`: the Jacobi identity for `C^2` fields (`localBracket_jacobi` in a chart:
    the second derivatives cancel by symmetry, the first-order terms by linearity);
  * `lieBracket_related`: if `X, Y` are `f`-related to `X', Y'` (`Tf ∘ X = X' ∘ f`), then
    `[X, Y]` is `f`-related to `[X', Y']` (Lang, Prop. V.1.4), for `f` of class `C^2`.
* [`LCManifolds/Derivation.lean`](LCManifolds/Derivation.lean): vector fields acting on
  functions.
  * `vfDeriv X φ`, the derivative `X(φ)` of a `C^1` function (`IsCkFunction`), and
    `vfDeriv_chart`: it does not depend on the chart; `vfDeriv_mul`: Leibniz rule;
  * `lieBracket_smulField`: `[X, φY] = X(φ) Y + φ [X, Y]`;
  * `eq_of_vfDeriv_clm_comp_chart`: a vector field is determined at `x` by its action on the
    local functions `λ ∘ c`, `λ` continuous linear (Hahn–Banach). Smooth bump functions need not
    exist, so global functions do not suffice in general.

There is no `sorry`.

## Design

Mathlib's `ChartedSpace` and `StructureGroupoid` accept an arbitrary topological model space, so
Mathlib's chart machinery is reused directly. A manifold modeled on `E` is a `ChartedSpace E M`
with `HasGroupoid M (mbGroupoid E)`. Only the notion of smoothness changes: Mathlib's
`contDiffGroupoid` needs a normed model with corners, and this library replaces it with the
Michal–Bastiani calculus of LCAnalysis.
